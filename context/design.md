# Design rules

Blog ideas and experiments lead the site. The portfolio is secondary.
Each project explanation gives the project, its problem, and its solution.
The article should be structured around four listed items: what problem
was had, which tools could solve it, how the tools were implemented or
how a solution came up (shared context), and how it looks and what it
allows.
Reading links must be easy to find.
Pictures must be very easy to understand.

Use Poppins for interface text and headings. Use Georgia for article text
until a suitable Minion Pro webfont licence is supplied. Preserve monospace code.
Article contents follow real heading levels. Do not invent subsections.
Respect reduced motion. Do not change section sizes during scroll.
Do not invent measured outcomes or production details in visual examples.
