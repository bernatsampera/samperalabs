All files are local drafts by Bernat Sampera, prepared with AI assistance. Publication dates are unset. Titles and descriptions come from [../journey.ts](../journey.ts), which contains six main steps and six optional branches. The main articles follow one export-guide example. This example is illustrative, not a recorded incident. The recorded handbook and reading tests remain separate evidence.

The matching story IDs in [../stories.json](../stories.json) identify the original source references and limits. [../metadata.json](../metadata.json) also includes supporting branch sources where a main article combines earlier experiments. Optional articles return to their parent step instead of adding stops to the main path.
