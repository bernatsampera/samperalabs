↗BJJGym

Find a BJJ gym.
Wherever you travel.

A way to find Brazilian jiu-jitsu gyms around the world, with the details that help you choose where to train.

WHAT IT IS

A gym discovery project for people who want to keep training while travelling.

PROBLEM

A name and an address are not enough. Useful details, such as no-gi training, clean mats, and teaching quality, are often missing from gym listings.

SOLUTION

I used AI to extract labels from public reviews and present useful information about each gym.

How I approached it

From public reviews to a better choice.

01 / FIND THE SIGNAL

Use what visitors share.

Public reviews contain clues about the training, the space, and the teachers. Those details are hard to compare when they sit inside long blocks of text.

02 / MAKE IT USEFUL

Extract clear labels.

Use AI to identify details such as BJJ, no-gi, cleanliness, teaching quality, and training level.

03 / HELP THE TRAVELLER

Present the gym's details.

Show this information with each gym so a visitor can understand what to expect before choosing where to train.

Design direction: explain the project first, then the problem and the solution.
