Guided journey: three examples

The same MAAT journey in three directions. No scrolling: use Next, the arrow keys, or "Jump to now". All dates and the migrations chapter are mock data.

  1. Flyover, version 2 (newest)Start with the three seasons. Enter one season and stay in it: its whole path, Start to Mission, with no map and no zooms.
  2. 1. Flyover mapA camera moves along one long route, like a travel tracker. Each season is a leg of the trip.
  3. 2. DocumentaryFull-screen scenes with large type. Each season opens like a new episode.
  4. 3. Departure boardA travel line with a moving marker. Each season opens on a flip-letter departure board.
