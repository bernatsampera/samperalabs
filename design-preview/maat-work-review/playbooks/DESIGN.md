# Approved operations

## Direction
Keep a fictional three-member list visible as the task moves through scope review, approval, and a bounded write. Two members change. One already paused member stays unchanged. Use a task sheet and outcome comparison.

Silent illustrated demonstration. Short on-screen sentences explain the purpose of each action. This is not a recording of a live run.

## Colors
Canvas #F2F2F4. Paper #FFFFFF. Ink #14141A. Secondary #5F5F67. Rule #E2E2E6. Blue #1D5BFF indicates the selected operation or data.

## Typography
Geist for interface and explanation. Monospace for data and code only. At 1920 by 1080, explanations use 62px and meaningful data uses at least 30px.

## Motion
Short dissolves maintain continuity. Reveal data in reading order. Hold complete states long enough to read. Use calm movement only when it explains a change.

## Do not use
No audio, logo intro, generic slogan, decoration, or invented measurements. No private identifiers. Do not turn the article into a series of text slides.
