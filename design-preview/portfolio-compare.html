BJJGym / Review classification

From many words
to a clearer mood.

A rating gives you a number. The review tells you how training felt. Make that feeling easier to find.

The engineering questionHow can a short label help someone read a review without losing its meaning?

This sketch shows a proposed review flow. All reviews and labels below are fictional samples.

Compare the same three samples

BeforeREAD EACH REVIEW
SAMPLE 01
“I was nervous on my first day. They made me feel part of the group.”
SAMPLE 02
“Hard rounds. Everyone pushed the pace. I left tired and happy.”
SAMPLE 03
“The coach was kind, but I felt lost during the class.”
AfterSCAN, THEN CHECK

01 / A sense of belongingWelcoming

The reviewer felt included on their first day.

“They made me feel part of the group.”

02 / A positive challengeEnergized

The effort was high. The feeling was positive.

“I left tired and happy.”

03 / Two feelings at onceMixed

Keep both the kind coach and the confusion.

“The coach was kind, but I felt lost.”

Both views use the same sample reviews. Labels describe these reviews, not the whole gym.

What makes it useful

The label is
only the start.

  1. 01
    Keep the original review.

    A short summary needs a path back to the words that support it.

  2. 02
    Allow mixed feelings.

    A review can praise one thing and question another. One positive label can hide that detail.

  3. 03
    Check the result against its source.

    Show the evidence beside the label. Let the reader judge whether the label fits.

Design proposal only. This preview does not call a model or process live reviews. It does not claim accuracy or show measured results.
