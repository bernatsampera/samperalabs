# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

AI engineers and technical peers who follow work on context and agents.
Companies and founders who could work with Bernat.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Bernat Sampera, an AI engineer who works on context.
It shows his current work as it happens.
A visit succeeds when the visitor understands his position, reads, returns, shares a chapter, or makes contact.

## Positioning

The work makes the case. The site shows real projects and real steps, not claims about skill.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Astro site. Article content comes mostly from SQLite.
- Existing public article URLs stay valid.
- Work is shown as journeys of dated chapters. New chapters are added as the work continues.

## Evidence on Hand

- Published articles on MAAT agents, the MAAT Brain, context trees, and global context.
- Public projects: BJJGym, IndoEuroMap.
- Do not invent outcomes, metrics, or production details.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work. Say little about it.
2. Every chapter must be clear to a reader who does not know the project.
3. Depth is optional. The full article is one step away, never required.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on all text. Respect reduced motion.
